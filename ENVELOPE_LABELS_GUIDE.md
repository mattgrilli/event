# Envelope Labels - Quick Guide

## What You Need

**Avery 5160 Labels** (or compatible)
- 30 labels per sheet (3 columns × 10 rows)
- Standard size: 1" × 2⅝"
- Available at: Staples, Office Depot, Amazon, Walmart
- Cost: ~$10-15 for 100 sheets (3,000 labels)
- Compatible brands: Any "5160 compatible" labels work

## How It Works

### Step 1: Generate Labels
1. Open the PTA Tickets app
2. Go to **Manage** tab
3. Click **"Print Envelope Labels"**
4. PDF will be generated automatically
5. Click "Yes" to open the PDF

### Step 2: Print
1. Load Avery 5160 label sheets into your printer
   - Print side UP (labels facing up in most printers)
   - Use manual feed or regular tray
2. Print the PDF
   - For 116 students = 4 sheets needed (120 labels total)
3. Let dry for 30 seconds if inkjet

### Step 3: Apply to Envelopes
1. Peel label from sheet
2. Stick on envelope
3. Done!

**Time estimate:** 
- Print: 5 minutes
- Apply: 20-30 minutes
- **Total: ~30 minutes** (vs 3+ hours handwriting)

## Pro Tips

✅ **Test print on regular paper first** - Make sure alignment is good before wasting label sheets

✅ **Print extra** - App generates one label per student (not per ticket), but print an extra sheet just in case

✅ **Centered text** - Labels are formatted for easy reading

✅ **Works for any event** - Change event name in Settings and generate new labels

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Labels don't line up with sheet
- **Solution:** Check printer settings - make sure "Actual Size" or "100% scale" is selected, NOT "Fit to Page"

**Problem:** Running low on ink
- **Solution:** Print in "Draft" or "Fast" mode - labels don't need high quality

**Problem:** Don't have Avery 5160?
- **Solution:** Most "address label" sheets are 5160 compatible. Check the package - if it says "30 labels per sheet" it'll probably work
